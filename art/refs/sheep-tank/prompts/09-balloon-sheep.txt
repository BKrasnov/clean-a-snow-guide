In-game screenshot mockup for a bright Roblox cartoon kids game about a sheep farm, third-person camera, chunky simple low-poly shapes, flat colours. The game goal: shear 1000 fluffy sheep. The player shears them on THEIR OWN cosy sheep farm: sunny green rolling hills, a little wooden shearing shed with a striped awning, white picket fences, round hay bales, sunflowers and daisies, fat wool sacks, a small wooden windmill, a winding dirt path, blue sky with puffy clouds. Summer, warm and cheerful, no snow anywhere. Sheep are cute cartoon sheep in natural colours: round white-cream wool, dark brown faces and legs, googly eyes, happy silly faces; a sheared sheep is small, smooth and pale pink-cream with a tiny tuft on its head, looking surprised and happy. On the farm stands the ENORMOUS red sheep tank exactly like the reference concept: round barrel tank with gold rivets and rims on stubby legs, a huge glass window showing hundreds of sheep packed inside, and a big grey metal pipe with a gold rim.
Idea: FLOATING FLUFF. The sheep are so fluffy that they float out of the pipe like balloons, drifting up into the sky over the farm. The player jumps on a springy trampoline pad and snips the floating sheep with long scissors; the wool pops into little clouds and the bald sheep parachute down gently with tiny umbrellas.
